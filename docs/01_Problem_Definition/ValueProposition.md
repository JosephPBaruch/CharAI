Value Proposition 

Biochar Placement Optimization Tool 

The Palouse region’s hilly terrain causes severe water runoff, stripping nutrients from the soil and forcing farmers to rely on costly fertilizer inputs tied to foreign natural gas markets. Biochar offers a promising solution by improving soil water retention and nutrient availability, but its high cost makes it important to apply only where it will have the greatest impact. Currently, farmers lack the tools to determine optimal placement, creating a knowledge gap that limits adoption. Our project addresses this gap by developing a full-stack application that generates prescription maps showing where biochar should be applied to maximize effectiveness. By aiding in efficient use of biochar, this tool will help farmers improve yields, reduce input costs, and decrease dependence on imported fertilizer, contributing to both economic and environmental resilience.
